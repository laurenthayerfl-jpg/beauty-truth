# The Beauty Truth Master Roadmap

**Last updated:** August 21, 2026  
**Document owner:** Lauren  
**Status:** Governing execution sequence, long-term strategy, and gate source of truth  
**Scope:** Product, business model, monetization, knowledge systems, and the relationship among the website, Codex, the future app, and future Beauty Truth products

## Purpose

The Beauty Truth is becoming an education-first skincare ecosystem, not simply a skincare blog, an ingredient scanner, an app, or a future product company.

The ecosystem is intended to include:

1. A trusted public skincare education platform.
2. A Codex-powered internal content and research operating system.
3. A structured Beauty Truth skincare knowledge base.
4. A consumer Beauty Truth app.
5. One paid Beauty Truth+ membership that spans the website and, eventually, the app and appropriate product benefits.
6. Affiliate and carefully controlled brand-partnership revenue.
7. Eventually, a Beauty Truth skincare product line, if consumer needs and brand trust justify it.

The governing strategic hierarchy is:

> **Trust first → education → useful tools → recurring relationship → products.**

Monetization must support the mission. It may not distort scientific conclusions, editorial judgment, recommendation logic, or the reader's understanding of uncertainty.

This roadmap describes direction and gates, not a fixed launch calendar. The active execution phase and its Definition of Done control what is worked on now. Longer-term strategic horizons may be documented for context, but they are not authorization to build ahead of the governing execution sequence.

## Governing Execution Sequence

**Decision:** Beauty Truth work follows this execution sequence:

> **IMPROVE → BUILD → OPERATE → REVIEW**

**Current active phase:** **IMPROVE**

The sequence is deliberately restrictive: first improve the existing website, then build only the approved growth system, then operate that system long enough to produce meaningful evidence, and only then review the evidence to decide whether more development is justified.

### Governing rule

> **Do not add new systems while the current phase's Definition of Done remains unmet unless the addition is required to complete that phase or Lauren explicitly directs otherwise.**

Research, planning, prototypes, vendor selection, integrations, infrastructure, and implementation can all become forms of premature system-building. They are therefore subject to this rule even when they appear useful. An item listed in a later strategic horizon remains deferred unless it is part of the approved current-phase scope or Lauren explicitly authorizes it.

### 1. IMPROVE — active

**Purpose:** Improve the existing Beauty Truth website before adding new growth systems.

The Improve phase is limited to:

- Website user experience and mobile experience.
- Technical SEO and site structure.
- Brand consistency.
- Trust and authority presentation.
- Article and readability experience.
- Priority existing-content improvements through the established preservation/content pipeline.
- A sufficient analytics and Google Search Console baseline to measure results.

The existing content pipeline may be used only for the defined priority existing-content batch and only when Lauren explicitly asks to run it for a topic, as required by `AGENTS.md` and `docs/website/content-pipeline.md`. This roadmap does not itself activate the pipeline or authorize publishing or WordPress changes.

#### Definition of Done — Improve

> **The existing Beauty Truth website is technically sound, professionally branded, easy to use, trustworthy, and its defined highest-priority existing content has been brought up to the current Beauty Truth standard.**

Improve does **not** require every legacy article to be updated before the phase can close. Lauren will define a bounded priority batch of existing content. The phase closes when the website-level work, that bounded content batch, and the measurement baseline above satisfy the Definition of Done and Lauren approves the gate.

### 2. BUILD — after Improve

Build does not begin until the Improve Definition of Done is met and Lauren approves the transition. The currently approved Build scope is limited to:

1. A Canva production system using approved professional templates.
2. Kit email/audience setup and ChatGPT/Codex integration.
3. The flagship Skincare Label Decoder through the appropriate evidence/content review process.
4. Distribution OS v1: approved article → 3 Pinterest assets + 1 Kit email + internal-link recommendations → Lauren approval.

Build is complete only after one article successfully travels through the complete production, publication, distribution, subscriber-acquisition, and measurement loop. Listing an item here defines the allowed Build scope; it does not independently authorize access to or changes in Canva, Kit, WordPress, ChatGPT, publishing, external accounts, paid services, or production systems. Those actions retain their existing approval requirements.

### 3. OPERATE — after Build

After Build is complete, operate the approved system for approximately 90 days. The purpose is to establish repeatable practice and collect actual workflow, audience, subscriber-acquisition, content, distribution, and measurement data.

Operate is not a continuation of Build. Do not keep adding systems, integrations, channels, or features simply because they may be useful. Changes during Operate should be limited to maintenance, corrections, safety or reliability needs, and adjustments required to run or measure the approved system, unless Lauren explicitly directs otherwise.

### 4. REVIEW — after Operate

After the operating period, use the actual performance and workflow data to decide whether additional development is justified. Review should identify what worked, what did not, where the workflow was costly or fragile, whether the measurement is credible, and which—if any—next investment has enough evidence to be authorized.

Review does not automatically authorize another system or strategic horizon. Any next scope requires an explicit Lauren decision, and the governing sequence begins again only with the newly approved scope and Definition of Done.

### Explicitly deferred while Improve is active

- All Build implementation, including the Canva production system, Kit setup and integration, the flagship Skincare Label Decoder, and Distribution OS v1, until the Improve gate is passed.
- Operate and Review work until their preceding gates are passed.
- Updating every legacy article; Improve requires only the bounded priority batch Lauren defines.
- New growth systems, channels, integrations, infrastructure, vendors, or paid services that are not required to complete Improve.
- Beauty Truth+ implementation, expanded affiliate or sponsorship systems, the structured knowledge base, the consumer app, product-market-fit testing, ecosystem scaling, and physical Beauty Truth products.
- Any unapproved feature, price, vendor, launch plan, partnership, publishing action, production change, or WordPress action described elsewhere in this roadmap.

Beauty Truth+ is one continuous membership ecosystem whose value grows as Beauty Truth grows.

| Stage | Intended member relationship | Candidate value | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website stage | Premium education and practical resources layered on top of a strong free public library | Product and formula deep dives, comparisons, routine-building resources, member-requested questions or analyses, and premium education | Working hypothesis; exact bundle requires validation |

Beauty Truth+ should initially be an education membership, not merely a paid blog. It should give members better ways to understand products, formulas, claims, and routines while leaving the majority of SEO acquisition content open and useful.

Pricing is a business hypothesis. Monthly versus annual plans, trials, limits, bundles, founding-member terms, and the free-to-paid boundary should be tested using consumer value, retention, willingness to pay, service cost, and contribution margin. No irreversible lifetime-pricing promise should be made without Lauren's deliberate approval and a financial analysis of its long-term obligations.

## Monetization and Editorial Independence

Potential revenue sources develop in this order:

1. Limited, editorially appropriate affiliate revenue.
2. Beauty Truth+ recurring membership revenue.
3. Carefully selected sponsorships, educational partnerships, or other appropriate media revenue.
4. Eventually, Beauty Truth skincare products if genuine unmet needs justify them.

The following rules apply across every execution phase and long-term strategic horizon:

- Commercial relationships must be clearly disclosed in plain language.
- Affiliate eligibility may not determine the scientific conclusion or whether a product is recommended.
- Sponsored content must be visibly distinguishable from independent editorial work.
- A sponsor may not preview, suppress, rewrite, or purchase the underlying scientific conclusion.
- Paid placement, if ever allowed, must be visibly labeled and kept out of the independent recommendation logic.
- Product inclusion criteria, comparison methods, conflicts, and material limitations should be documented.
- Beauty Truth must be willing to decline revenue that would weaken trust or create hidden preferential treatment.
- Aggregate commercial performance may inform what consumers find useful, but it must not be used as evidence that a scientific claim is true.

## App Concept and Economic Model

The consumer app should answer a richer question than “Is this ingredient good or bad?”

```text
Product and formulation context
+ user profile
+ existing routine
+ preferences and goals
+ Beauty Truth knowledge
= personalized explanation and decision support
```

Routine preferences should recognize different desired levels of complexity, including minimalist, moderate, and enthusiast users. More steps are not automatically better, and the app should help consumers identify duplication and decide whether they actually need a product.

Candidate MVP capabilities include product or barcode scanning, product identification, formula explanations, Beauty Truth AI conversation, a saved skincare routine, routine personality and preferences, product comparisons, “Do I actually need this?” analysis, and personalized routine compatibility. These are candidates to prioritize through research; this list is not approval to build every feature into the first release.

### Cost categories to measure

- Hosting and backend services.
- Database and storage.
- Product-data acquisition, licensing, normalization, and maintenance.
- AI and other API usage.
- Monitoring and analytics.
- App-store registration, review, and transaction costs.
- Privacy, legal, accessibility, and potential external technical or security review.
- Customer support, content maintenance, and scientific review.

No reliable cost amounts are currently documented. Costs must be estimated from actual requirements and then replaced with measured costs during prototypes and beta testing.

### Cost-control architecture principles

- Store and retrieve reusable Beauty Truth knowledge and previously analyzed products when licensing and freshness rules permit.
- Use deterministic database retrieval for known facts instead of asking AI to rediscover them.
- Preserve source provenance, version, review status, and freshness so reuse does not turn outdated information into false certainty.
- Use lower-cost models for bounded tasks when quality testing shows they are adequate.
- Reserve more capable reasoning for requests whose ambiguity or complexity actually requires it.
- Cache only when privacy, accuracy, permissions, and freshness make caching appropriate.
- Measure usage and cost by feature, free user, active user, and paid member before setting permanent limits or pricing.
- Design graceful limits and non-AI fallbacks so growth does not automatically create unsustainable AI expense.

The core economic measures are AI cost per active user, AI cost per paid member, total infrastructure cost, free-to-paid conversion, retention, revenue per user, and contribution margin. Revenue alone is not sufficient; a product can grow revenue while losing money on each additional engaged user.

## Strategic Horizon 7 — Beauty Truth Skincare

**Horizon status:** Deferred future idea and conditional strategic direction; no physical product is approved for development.

### What we are building

Only after sufficient audience trust and consumer understanding, Beauty Truth may investigate physical skincare products that address demonstrated unmet needs. Any product should follow the principles of formulation over marketing, barrier-first design, evidence-informed claims, purposeful rather than redundant use, long-term skin function, and transparent education without fear-mongering.

### Why we are building it

Aggregated consumer questions, product gaps, routine difficulties, and app/content insights may reveal needs that existing products do not serve well. Product development is justified only when Beauty Truth can create meaningful consumer value—not simply because the brand has an audience.

### Codex agents and systems involved

- Content, evidence, claims-review, knowledge, app, and analytics systems to organize privacy-respecting evidence of need.
- Main Codex task for research organization, requirements, documentation, risk tracking, and decision support when explicitly authorized.
- Qualified human formulators, manufacturers, safety assessors, regulatory/legal reviewers, quality specialists, and other professionals as required.
- Independent editorial and recommendation governance so commercial ownership does not predetermine recommendations.

### Consumer-facing outcome

Purposeful, understandable products that solve a validated need, fit the brand's education, and are held to the same evidence and recommendation standards as third-party products.

### Monetization introduced

Physical product revenue. Beauty Truth+ may eventually include approved benefits such as early access, member pricing, samples, or other useful perks. Membership should not pressure members into buying products they do not need.

### Estimated or known costs

No product or budget is approved. Future analysis must include formulation and samples, stability/compatibility and preservative testing as applicable, safety and regulatory review, claims substantiation, packaging, minimum order quantities, manufacturing, quality control, insurance, inventory, warehousing, fulfillment, returns, customer support, marketing, and working capital.

### Success metrics

- Strength and frequency of the validated unmet consumer need.
- Product performance, tolerability, quality, claims support, and customer satisfaction.
- Repeat purchase where appropriate to actual product use—not forced consumption.
- Return, complaint, adverse-event, defect, and support rates.
- Gross margin, inventory risk, cash requirements, and contribution margin.
- Independent evidence that the product earns recommendation consideration without preferential rules.
- Effect on long-term trust and member relationships.

### Gate before advancing

No product should move from exploration to commercial development until Lauren approves a specific, repeatedly observed unmet need; evidence and competitive assessment; product brief; qualified development partners; regulatory and safety path; claims plan; quality and testing plan; supply-chain feasibility; full cost and cash-risk model; and conflict-of-interest controls. No launch should occur until the finished product passes the applicable safety, stability, quality, claims, manufacturing, usability, and economic gates.

### Major risks and unanswered questions

- Owning products creates an unavoidable commercial conflict that must be disclosed and governed.
- Inventory, cash, formulation, testing, regulation, manufacturing, and liability risks are materially different from media or software risks.
- Consumer insight may reveal a need that Beauty Truth is not uniquely qualified or economically able to solve.
- Product benefits, launch channel, assortment, pricing, manufacturers, claims, and timing are entirely undecided.
- Beauty Truth products must earn their place in recommendations just as third-party products do.
